VectraMail Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

What VectraMail accesses

When you sign in with Google, VectraMail requests the scopes https://mail.google.com/, openid and email. They are used only to identify which Gmail address you signed in with and to read, organize and send that account's email over IMAP and SMTP at your request.

Where your data goes

  • VectraMail runs entirely on your Mac. It has no backend server; mail data is transferred only between your Mac and Google's mail servers.
  • Downloaded messages are stored in a local database on your Mac.
  • OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac and are never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • VectraMail does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to any third party, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use

VectraMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove an account in VectraMail at any time, which deletes its local data and tokens. You can also revoke VectraMail's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
